## 7. Scenarios & Actionable Insights

**Bullish (base case, given trend alignment + positive momentum):**
A decisive daily close **above 336** (clearing the 333–336 supply cluster) would confirm the Bollinger squeeze resolution and open the path to **337–340**, then **344–346**. Traders could initiate/add on such a confirmed breakout, using the 322/317 support zone (or ~1.5×ATR ≈ 12 pts below entry) as risk definition.

**Pullback / buy-the-dip:**
Given price is only marginally above the upper band and ATR is ~8 points, a mean-reversion dip toward the **317–322 confluence** is plausible and would be a higher-quality entry than chasing at 332. A hold of the 50 SMA/VWMA zone on lighter volume would keep the trend structure intact and offer a defined-risk long (stop below ~316, the Bollinger mid).

**Bearish invalidation:**
A failure at 333–336 that closes back **below 322 (10 EMA)**, and especially a loss of **317.5–316.5** (50 SMA / Bollinger mid), would signal the squeeze resolved lower. Below that, the market would likely test **308–310**, then **301–302**. Note the precedent: two prior approaches into the 328–340 zone (2026-07-15/17 and 2026-07-24/30) were followed by sharp reversals — including the 2026-07-31 gap down — so the 333–340 region deserves respect, not complacency.

**Nuanced risk notes:**
- Price was rejected from this same ~333–340 neighborhood at the end of July, so a **break-and-hold**, not just an intraday poke, is the confirmation signal.
- MACD (3.27) and RSI (62.84) are both **below** their July extremes (9.60 / 71.66), meaning this rally is so far less momentum-rich than the July leg.
- The 200 SMA at 284.54 is far below price (+16.8%), which supports the long-term trend but also highlights that the stock is extended versus its long-term mean.
