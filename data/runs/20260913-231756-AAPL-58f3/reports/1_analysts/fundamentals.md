### Key balance-sheet takeaways

2. **Retained earnings crossed from negative to positive** (–$14.3B Sep-25 → **+$11.3B Jun-26**), lifting equity **+46% in 9 months** to $107.5B. This is the mechanical result of net income ($101.5B in 9M FY26) exceeding combined buybacks + dividends.

This is the single most important item to monitor at the FY26 Q4 print.
4. **Working capital collapsed to nearly zero ($0.49B from $9.47B in March).** The build was financed by **stretching payables to $64.5B** (DPO ~107 days vs. ~91 a year ago) — suppliers, not Apple's balance sheet, are funding the inventory. Days sales outstanding are stable at ~26 days, so receivables growth (+25.8% YoY) is roughly in line with revenue and **not** a channel-stuffing alarm. Cash conversion cycle remains a favorable **–63 days**.
5. **UNRESOLVED — $20.3B of Goodwill & Intangibles** appears in Mar-26/Jun-26 but is absent in all prior periods (where Tangible Book Value = Stockholders' Equity). Apple historically carries negligible goodwill. Either (a) a large acquisition closed in Q1/Q2 CY2026, (b) the vendor reclassified items, or (c) a data error. **No "Purchase of Business" line appears in the 2026 quarterly cash-flow statements**, which argues against a cash-funded M&A deal — but this must be verified before relying on tangible-book metrics. Note it reduces tangible book value by 19% ($107.5B equity → $87.2B tangible).

## 6. Actionable Insights for Traders

**Bullish evidence (specific):**
1. Revenue growth accelerated to **+16.4% YoY** in Q3 FY26, with the Dec-25 quarter a record **$143.76B** — a decisive break from the FY22–FY24 plateau.
2. **Gross margin of 50.06%**, up 357 bps YoY and up ~680 bps from FY22 — mix and pricing power are working.
3. **TTM FCF $136.7B (+38% YoY)** and capital returns fully self-funded (72% of FCF vs. 107% in FY25).
4. **$48B of debt retired in 4 years**; broad net cash improved ~$28B in 9 months to ~$62B.
5. **Retained earnings/equity inflection** (–$14.3B → +$11.3B retained earnings; equity +46% in 9 months) supports continued buybacks without balance-sheet strain.

**Bearish/watch-list evidence (specific):**
1. **Inventory +87% YoY (+64% QoQ) to $11.09B** — pre-launch fill or demand risk; the highest-priority item at the FY26 Q4 print.
2. **R&D +32% YoY** ($11.73B, 10.72% of revenue) — an AI arms race that will compress margins if revenue growth slows below ~10%.
3. **50% gross margin may be peak**; lapping it in FY27 gets progressively harder, and tariff/refund effects are not identifiable from this data.
4. **Unexplained $20.3B goodwill/intangibles** — verify before trusting tangible-book or any M&A narrative.
5. **Working capital ~$0.5B** and **payables stretched to $64.5B** — a thin liquidity buffer that relies on supplier terms.
6. **Comparability trap:** FY25's $7.46 EPS and 15.6% tax rate were flattered by FY24's one-time EU charge; the underlying pre-charge growth rate is lower than headline.

**Positioning logic:** The fundamentals favor a **constructive/accumulate stance on strength** — the operating inflection, FCF surge, and deleveraging are all confirmed by three independent statements. However, the inventory build and record gross margin create **asymmetric downside into the FY26 Q4 print (~late Oct/early Nov 2026)**, and the withheld valuation data means entry price must be validated externally. Tactically, this argues for a **BUY bias on confirmed healthy sell-through / inventory normalization**, and caution if inventory rises again in Q4.

Apple's fundamentals as of 2026-09-11 are the **strongest in at least five years**: high-teens revenue growth, 50%+ gross margins, ~$8.74 TTM EPS (+30%), $136.7B TTM FCF (+38%), $48B of debt retired in four years, and a **net-cash balance sheet with the broad net cash position up ~$28B in nine months**.
