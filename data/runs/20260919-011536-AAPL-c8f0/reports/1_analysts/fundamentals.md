**Cautious factors (evidence-based):**

3. **Extreme P/B (41.4x) is a buyback artifact**, not a quality measure — don't read it as either a bargain or a distress signal.
4. **Missing forward data** — vendor does not serve forward estimates/beta/dividend yield here, so forward-multiple and risk-adjusted comparisons can't be made from this dataset.

**Practical framing:**
- **Momentum/quality buyers:** Fundamentals support a *hold-to-accumulate-on-weakness* posture given the growth inflection, margin expansion, and cash strength. The fundamentals do not argue for chasing strength at 36x.
- **Valuation-sensitive traders:** The premium multiple means position sizing and risk management matter more than usual. A growth print below the recent ~15% YoY pace is the key downside catalyst.
- **Key monitoring triggers:** (a) next quarterly revenue growth vs. the ~16% YoY run-rate, (b) gross-margin trajectory around the 49% level, (c) OCF/FCF trend, (d) share-count reduction (buyback pace), (e) any reversal in the equity-base build.
