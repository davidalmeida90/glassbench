Where I disagree is the conclusion. The fact that it is aggressive does not make it smart. For a conservative mandate, the correct adjustment is not to applaud the hold, but to reduce the exposure that makes the hold dangerous.

NVDA has a 2.22 beta, and it is one of the longest-duration assets in the S&P 500. The forward PE of 14 and PEG of 0.46 depend on forward EPS nearly doubling from $7.90 to $15.57. If rates rise another 15 to 20 basis points and those estimates do not move, the multiple compresses. The earnings engine may outrun the discount rate for a while, but that is not a guarantee. We protect the firm. We do not bet the firm on the earnings engine winning a race against the bond market.
