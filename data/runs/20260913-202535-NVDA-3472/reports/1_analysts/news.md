## 1. Bottom Line Up Front

Against that, `NVDA`'s **fundamental AI-demand narrative is accelerating, not cracking** (Anthropic's $517B compute lock-in, "monster" earnings from `NVDA` and Broadcom, a $99B investment portfolio, Goldman's "AI = half of S&P 500 earnings growth"). Bubble-pop odds remain modest at 12%.

**Net: I lean HOLD into the Sept 16 event — defense over offense for the next 72 hours**, with clear asymmetry (hawkish surprise = trim; dovish hold = add).
