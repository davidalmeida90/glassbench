That is exactly where a prudent plan can drift into opportunistic risk-taking.

The lower Bollinger band is 171.40, the December low is 170.54, and the 200-SMA is 161.79. From 184.43, those are real downside levels. From an add at 186, they are worse. Require stronger confirmation, such as a weekly close above 190.09 with the MACD histogram expanding, or a breakout above 186 that successfully retests and holds 186. We can always add after the breakout is proven. We cannot undo a bad add into a failed breakout. I support HOLD, but a defensive hold with stricter add criteria, not a loaded hold.
