## 3. Actionable Insights & Scenarios

**Scenario A — Bullish continuation / breakout**
- Trigger: a daily close **above the upper Bollinger band at 304.51** (and above the 2026-05-15 high of 302.94) on above-average volume.
- Rationale: trend + MACD + VWMA all aligned; RSI overbought is characteristic of strong trends.
- Risk control: trail stops around the 10 EMA (291.35) or 1.5×ATR (~290.62); reduce size given overbought RSI.

**Scenario B — Healthy pullback / buy-the-dip**
- Watch zones: **10 EMA 291.35** (first), **VWMA 284.95** (second), **Bollinger middle 281.11** (third).
- A pullback that holds these levels while MACD stays positive and RSI cools toward 50–60 would represent a higher-quality, less-extended entry.
- Deeper support: **50 SMA 265.74**, then **200 SMA 258.43** (structural trend floor).

**Scenario C — Overbought blow-off / reversal warning**
- Warning signs: a bearish RSI divergence (price higher high, RSI lower high), MACD histogram rolling over, or a decisive close **back below the 10 EMA / VWMA**.
- A failure at the $300–304.51 resistance shelf would raise the probability of a retest of the middle band (~281) or the 50 SMA (~265.74).

**Key takeaway:** The trend is unambiguously up and momentum confirms it, but the stock is **extended (RSI 76, ~13% above the 50 SMA, near the upper band)**. This favors *managing* existing long exposure with trailing stops rather than *chasing* new longs at the highs. New entries are better staged on controlled pullbacks into the 291/285/281 support cluster, or on a confirmed volume-backed breakout above 304.51.
