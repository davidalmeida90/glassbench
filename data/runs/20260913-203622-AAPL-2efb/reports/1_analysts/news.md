## 5. Actionable Trading Insights for `AAPL`

1. **Event risk is binary and imminent: FOMC Sept 16.** With an 78% priced hike, a *hike* is largely discounted — but the **dot plot/guidance** is the real risk. A hawkish "more to come" tone pushes the 10Y toward/above 5.0% and pressures `AAPL`'s multiple. **Do not add indiscriminately into the print.**
2. **`AAPL`'s product catalyst is a genuine offset.** Foldable iPhone Duo on sale this week + pricing power to absorb memory inflation + "near buy point" flag + Berkshire backing give `AAPL` relative resilience vs. pure high-multiple AI names. `AAPL` is likely to **outperform the average long-duration tech name** in a hawkish-Fed tape.
3. **Watch the 10Y and oil as the key triggers.** `AAPL`'s direction this week is more a function of **10Y (4.80→4.95) and Brent/WTI toward $100** than of company news. A 10Y >5.0% is a clear de-risking signal for mega-cap tech; a pullback with oil/yields would be the "buy the buy-point" window.
4. **Position sizing over direction.** Low recession odds (6%) and secular demand argue against an outright bearish stance; the hawkish rates regime argues against aggressive adds. Favor holding a core position, using the FOMC/product-launch volatility to **scale in on weakness** rather than chase.
5. **Monitor the foldable's Oct-availability slip.** The Polymarket "by Sept 30" collapse to 1% suggests sell-through recognition may land in **Q4/FY27**, so near-term revenue beats may be modest — temper expectations for an immediate "supercycle" earnings pop.
