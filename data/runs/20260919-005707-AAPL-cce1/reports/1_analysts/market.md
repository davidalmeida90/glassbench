6. **MACD needs confirmation.** The **histogram at −0.01** is a razor-thin bearish crossover. If it widens negative in coming sessions, it corroborates continued downside momentum; if it flips back positive quickly, the 2/12–2/13 drop may prove to be a shakeout.

   - **Bullish repair:** Reclaim 261.62 (mid-band) and then 266–267 (10 EMA/50 SMA) on strong volume → restores the uptrend narrative and re-opens a test of the ~275–277 highs.
