## 1. Executive Summary

INTC is in the late stage of an extraordinary, multi-month parabolic advance that has now shown its **first decisive short-term breakdown**. The stock closed at **$108.77** on 2026-05-15 (verified snapshot), having peaked at a **$129.44 close on 2026-05-11** — a **−15.97% decline in just four trading sessions**. This is a classic "strong trend meets blow-off risk" configuration: the trend-followers' backdrop is bullish while the tactical setup is fragile.

**Selected indicators (8) and why:** `close_50_sma` + `close_200_sma` (medium/long-term trend & golden-cross context), `close_10_ema` (short-term momentum shift — critical right now as price just lost it), `macd` (momentum crossover/deceleration), `rsi` (overbought/divergence), `boll_ub` (parabolic extension gauge), `atr` (volatility & risk sizing), `vwma` (volume-weighted trend confirmation).
