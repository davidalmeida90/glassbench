**Recommendation**: Hold

**Rationale**:

The MACD has crossed bearishly, lower highs are forming, and heavy-volume down sessions point to short-term distribution. The upcoming Q3 FY26 earnings print is unresolved and binary.

At the same time, the intact medium-term trend, strong margins, platform dominance, and balance-sheet strength make a bearish Underweight or Sell too aggressive. The evidence is balanced enough to leave existing exposure unchanged and avoid new capital until one side proves itself.
