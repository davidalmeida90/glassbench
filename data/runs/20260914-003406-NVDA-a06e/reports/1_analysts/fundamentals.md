## 4. Profitability & Returns

- **FY2025 ROE ≈ 119%** — net income $72.88B ÷ average equity of ~$61.2B. Extraordinary, though flattered by a shrunken equity base after aggressive buybacks.
- **FY2025 ROA ≈ 82%** — net income $72.88B ÷ average total assets of ~$88.7B.
- **FY2025 ROIC ≈ >100%** — NOPAT (EBIT $84.3B × (1−13.3%)) ≈ $73.1B vs. average invested capital ~$70.2B.
- **Effective tax rate FY2025 = 13.3%** — low versus statutory, aided by favorable tax items; Q2 FY2026 ran ~15.3%. A rising tax rate is a modest forward headwind to net margin.
- **Deferred tax assets ballooned to $13,570M** (from $10,979M at FY2025 year-end) — a growing future tax shield.

**Insight:** Operating leverage is being fully captured — incremental revenue is dropping to the bottom line at very high rates. But the ROE figures are optically inflated by buyback-reduced book equity, not by leverage (the company is net-cash).

## 8. Actionable Insights for Traders

1. **Core business is firing on all cylinders.** A ~$46.7B quarter with 60.8% operating and 56.5% net margins confirms the AI-compute supercycle is intact at the fundamental level. Revenue and earnings have compounded >100% YoY for two consecutive years.

2. **Strip out non-operating gains.** Q2 EPS of $1.08 includes ~$2.25B of securities gains. On a normalized basis, net income was ~$24.5B (≈7% lower). For a clean read on operating momentum, use the $28.4B operating income line, not headline net income.

3. **Watch working capital as the leading demand indicator.** Inventory (+48% since year-end to ~106 days) and receivables (+21%, ~54 DSO) are building faster than the revenue base. This is the single most important forward signal: continued build is either (a) demand-driven ramp (bullish) or (b) channel stuffing (bearish). Monitor next quarter's inventory/receivable turns and the change in working capital drag.

4. **Cash-flow conversion temporarily depressed.** OCF/NI of ~58% in Q2 means cash generation lagged accounting earnings materially. FCF of $13.5B is still huge, but the gap warrants watching — if working capital normalizes, OCF should snap back sharply.

5. **Balance sheet is a buffer, not a constraint.** ~$43.4B net cash, 4.2× current ratio, and 10.6% debt/equity mean NVDA can fund aggressive growth, buybacks, and withstand a demand air-pocket without external financing. Low solvency risk.

6. **Tax rate is a mild headwind.** FY2025's 13.3% effective rate was unusually low; Q2 ran 15.3%. A normalization toward the high-teens would trim net margin modestly.

7. **Valuation is the missing piece.** Because the profile dataset withheld market-cap and multiples, traders must combine these exceptional fundamentals with a live price to judge whether the growth is already discounted. The fundamentals argue for a premium multiple; whether the *current* price already embeds it cannot be assessed from this dataset.

8. **Overall fundamental stance:** Strongly constructive. Growth, margins, cash flow, and balance sheet all point to a high-quality compounder with no imminent fundamental deterioration. The main risks are (i) demand-cycle sensitivity, (ii) working-capital build, and (iii) valuation/expectations risk (not measurable here).
