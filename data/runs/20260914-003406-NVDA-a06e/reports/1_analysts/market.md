## 7. Actionable Insights

1. **Constructive bias with confirmation pending.** The 10 EMA > 50 SMA > 200 SMA stack plus the fresh 10/50 crossover favors the bull case. But since the MACD histogram is still slightly negative, treat this as *accumulation on strength*, not an all-in signal.

2. **Breakout trigger:** A close above **180** (clearing the Sep 10–11 highs) with above-average volume would confirm the next leg, opening **184–185** (Aug high / upper band). A MACD/signal bullish crossover coinciding with that would be a high-quality entry.

3. **Pullback entry:** If NVDA retests the **173.7–174.5** cluster (50 SMA / 10 EMA / VWMA) and holds, that is a favorable risk-defined long entry.

4. **Risk management:** Using ATR (4.82), a stop near **170** (≈1.5× ATR) sits just below Sep 2–4 congestion; a wider stop near **167.75** (2× ATR) sits below the Sep 5 low and above the lower band — a deeper invalidation level.

5. **Thesis invalidation:** A decisive close below **166** (Sep 5 low / lower band) would break the recovery structure and shift the short-term bias bearish, likely targeting a deeper 50 SMA test and signaling the MACD rollover was not a false dip.

6. **Watch for:** (a) MACD histogram turning positive, (b) volume confirmation on any 180 break, (c) RSI holding above 50. These three would collectively shift the setup from *recovery* to *trend continuation*.
