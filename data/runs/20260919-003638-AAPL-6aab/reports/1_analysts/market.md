- **Suggested stop discipline:** ~266 (1×ATR) for tactical longs; ~261 (2×ATR, above lower band) for swing positions; ~255 (50 SMA) as the strategic line-in-the-sand.

**Position management note:** With RSI at 65 and MACD crossing down, this is a *poor* spot for aggressive new longs near 272 — better to **wait for either a dip into the 267–270 support cluster or a confirmed breakout above 275**.
