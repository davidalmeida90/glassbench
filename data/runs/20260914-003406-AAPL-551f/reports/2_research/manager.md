**Recommendation**: Underweight

**Rationale**: This was a strong debate, but the bear landed the more decisive blows on valuation and near-term risk/reward. The bull is right that AAPL remains an elite-quality franchise: price is above the 10-EMA, 50-SMA, 200-SMA, and VWMA; RSI has reset to neutral; the 50/200-SMA gap is nearly closed; gross margin hit 46.5%; quarterly FCF was ~$24.4B; and the buyback has retired ~8.4% of shares. Those facts argue against an outright Sell. However, the bear's arithmetic is the problem the bull never adequately rebutted. At $233.21 against FY2024 EPS of $6.08, AAPL trades at roughly 38x trailing earnings, even though revenue and net income remain below FY2022 levels and EPS has been flat for three years. The bull waved off valuation as a data gap, but the bear showed it is calculable and demanding. Meanwhile, the technical picture is not clean: MACD has crossed down with a negative histogram, the September 12 rebound came on lower volume than the September 10 selloff, and the "imminent" golden cross is unconfirmed and arriving after an 18.6% five-week rally — often a late-stage signal rather than a fresh catalyst. The near-term setup is also poor: roughly +2.8% to the upper Bollinger band at 239.78 versus about -5.6% to the 220-222 support cluster. The bull wins on quality and structural trend, but the bear wins on price, momentum, and margin of safety. That combination supports a cautious, trim-into-strength posture rather than buying or outright exiting.

**Strategic Actions**: Trader should reduce AAPL to an underweight allocation relative to benchmark/neutral exposure. Concretely: if AAPL is currently a full benchmark weight, trim to roughly 50-75% of that weight; if it is already an overweight, take more off. Use strength toward the 236-240 resistance zone — specifically the 239-240 area and upper Bollinger band at 239.78 — to sell into rallies, and do not add new long exposure at 233-236 into overhead supply. For any retained core position, tighten stops to the 224-226 zone (below the September 10 low of 225.12), with a structural invalidation at a decisive close below the 220-222 SMA/Bollinger cluster; a close below that zone would justify reducing further or exiting. The underweight thesis would need reassessment only on a decisive, higher-volume close above 240 that breaks the current resistance structure. Do not initiate short exposure based on this debate; the recommendation is to trim and avoid chasing, not to press the downside against an intact primary uptrend.
