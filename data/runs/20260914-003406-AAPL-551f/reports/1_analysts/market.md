## 6. Actionable Insights

**Base case — bullish trend intact, short-term momentum cooling.** The dominant signal is the **multi-timeframe uptrend plus the imminent golden cross**, confirmed by price sitting above the VWMA. The pullback looks like normal profit-taking inside an uptrend rather than a reversal, and the 09-12 candle is a constructive reclaim.

2. **Risk control (ATR-based):** With ATR = 4.72, a tactical stop ~1.5–2× ATR below entry (~**224–226**) sits below the 09-10 low (225.12). A wider structural stop belongs under the **50/200-SMA cluster ≈ 220** and the lower band (221.93), which is the line in the sand for the trend thesis.
3. **Momentum caveat:** Because the **MACD histogram is negative (−0.93)**, avoid chasing strength blindly — scale in / wait for the reclaim of overhead supply, or for MACD to curl back above its signal, to reduce the risk of buying into a deeper consolidation.
4. **Position sizing:** Elevated but moderating volatility (ATR 4.72) argues for **smaller-than-average size** with wider stops, or options structures that define risk, rather than tight stops that a normal daily range would clip.
5. **What would break the thesis:** A decisive close below the **220 SMA cluster / 221.93 lower band**, especially with RSI turning down from ~57 and MACD continuing lower, would invalidate the golden-cross setup and shift the bias to neutral/bearish.
