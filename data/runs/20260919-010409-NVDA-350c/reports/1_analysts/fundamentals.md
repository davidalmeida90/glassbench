## 1. Executive Summary

NVIDIA's filed fundamentals show a company still in **hyper-growth with elite profitability and a fortress balance sheet**. The trailing-twelve-month (TTM) figures built exclusively from SEC filings show **revenue of $187.1B, net income of $99.2B (53.0% net margin), and free cash flow of $77.3B**. The most recent quarter (Q3 FY2026, ended 2025-10-26) delivered **$57.0B in revenue, +62.5% YoY and +21.9% QoQ** — an *acceleration* off an already extraordinary base.

The equity trades at **$182.81** (2026-02-13 close), a **$4.44T market cap**, and **44.8x TTM earnings / 39.0x EV/EBITDA / 23.7x sales**. That is a premium multiple, but it sits against ~60% top-line growth, ~100% ROE, and a **$52B net-cash position**. The principal near-term catalyst is the **Q4 FY2026 report expected in late February 2026** — after this analysis date.

**Stance:** Fundamentally **constructive/bullish**. Valuation is rich in absolute terms and leaves little room for execution error, so this is a "quality-growth-at-a-premium" setup where position sizing and event risk (earnings, China export policy) matter as much as direction.

## 9. Actionable Insights for Traders

**Bull case (evidence-backed):**
1. **Growth is re-accelerating, not decelerating** — Q3 FY2026 revenue +62.5% YoY / +21.9% QoQ, with EPS up 65% YoY. The "peak growth" thesis is not supported by the latest filings.
2. **Margins have recovered** from the Q1 FY2026 export-charge trough (60.5% → 73.4% GM), and operating leverage is expanding margins (63.2% op margin in Q3 FY2026).
3. **Balance-sheet optionality is enormous** — ~$52B net cash and ~7% debt/equity mean NVDA can fund R&D, absorb policy shocks, and keep buying back stock without dilution risk.
4. **Valuation is defensible on a growth-adjusted basis** (PEG ~0.7–0.8x) with ~100% ROE and 41% FCF margins.
5. **Capital returns are aggressive** (FY2025 financing outflow $42.4B) — a persistent buyback bid that supports EPS growth.

**Bear case / risks:**
1. **Valuation is priced for perfection** — 44.8x TTM PE, 39x EV/EBITDA, 23.7x sales, and only a **1.74% FCF yield**. Any guidance miss is likely punished hard.
2. **Q4 FY2026 earnings is imminent (late February 2026)** — a binary, high-volatility event that occurs *after* this analysis date. Positioning into it is a judgment call, not a fundamentals call.
3. **China/export-control policy remains the key structural swing factor** — the Q1 FY2026 gross-margin collapse to 60.5% demonstrates how quickly policy can hit reported results.
4. **Cash conversion below 1x (OCF/NI 0.84x)** — heavy working-capital build; if revenue growth slows sharply, watch for inventory/receivable write-down risk.
5. **No dividend support** — the token $0.01/share payout provides no downside cushion; returns depend entirely on the equity price.

**Suggested framing for a trader:**
- **Trend/momentum traders:** The filing data supports the uptrend — growth accelerating, margins expanding, balance sheet pristine. But the stock is expensive; size positions for a high-beta earnings event.
- **Value-conscious investors:** Wait for a valuation reset (a multiple compression toward the low-30s PE) or a fundamental wobble to build a position; the 1.74% TTM FCF yield offers no margin of safety at current prices.
- **Risk management:** Key monitorables into Q4 FY2026 — (a) sequential revenue growth durability, (b) gross margin guidance (~74%+ to confirm the export drag is behind), (c) any China policy commentary, and (d) working-capital/cash-conversion trend.
