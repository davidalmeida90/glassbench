The bull counters these points with reasonable context but does not disprove them; the bear concedes the franchise quality but argues the stock price already reflects near-perfection. With strong structural quality offset by unconfirmed working-capital/cash-conversion deterioration and a neutral-to-cautious tape, the evidence does not clearly justify adding or exiting exposure here. The right stance is to hold existing NVDA positions and wait for confirmation rather than force a direction.
