- **Operating income:** **$683M**, the first positive operating quarter in about two years, after a Q2 2025 operating loss of $(3,176)M. That’s a **$3.86B sequential swing**.
- **9M2025 operating income** is still negative at $(2,794)M, but the trajectory is unmistakably up.

The bear wants to say “the turnaround isn’t confirmed.” Fair. But markets discount trajectories, not rear-view snapshots. When gross margin expands 10.7 points in one quarter and operating income flips positive, you don’t wait for three more quarters of confirmation. You accumulate INTC near support.

### 2. The Balance Sheet Has Been De-Risked — Liquidity Fear Is Dead

The single biggest bear argument against INTC was solvency risk from the fab buildout. That argument is now much weaker:

- **Cash & equivalents:** $11.141B.
- **Current ratio:** **1.60**, up from 1.24 in Q2 2025.
- **Stockholders’ equity:** **$106.376B**, up **+$8.5B QoQ**.
- **Liabilities / Equity:** **~0.92x**, improving.
- **Total assets:** **$204.514B**.

Where did that come from? The **~$15–16B of strategic equity infusions in 2025**: the US government’s ~10% stake, SoftBank’s $2B, and NVIDIA’s $5B. That is not distress financing. That is the US government, SoftBank, and NVIDIA — three of the most powerful capital allocators on earth — buying into Intel’s future.

Bear, if you want to call that dilution, fine. I call it **strategic validation at a price that shored up the balance sheet and funded the 18A/14A roadmap**. Dilution is a cost. Insolvency is a death sentence. Intel chose correctly.

### 3. Competitive Advantages: Intel Is a National Asset, Not a Sinking Ship

The bear will say Intel lost to AMD and NVIDIA. But look at what just happened:

- **NVIDIA’s $5B investment comes with an x86/AI partnership.** That is NVIDIA — the undisputed AI leader — explicitly validating Intel’s x86 franchise in the AI era. That’s not a sympathy vote.
- **The US government took a ~10% stake.** Intel is now a strategic national asset for semiconductor reshoring and defense. That creates a moat no pure-play foundry can replicate.
- **SoftBank invested $2B.** Another deep-pocketed strategic partner.
- **Intel is executing portfolio rationalization:** divesting a majority stake in Altera, selling IMS Nanofabrication, and canceling European fab projects to cut CapEx. That’s capital discipline, not chaos.
- **New CEO Lip-Bu Tan** is running a cost and portfolio reset. The Q2 2025 loss was inflated by restructuring charges. The Q3 operating profit shows the underlying business can respond.

The foundry opportunity — 18A/14A, US-based leading-edge capacity, advanced packaging — is enormous. The bear says “show me the customers.” I say the strategic capital and NVIDIA partnership are exactly the precursors to those customer wins. You don’t get the US government and NVIDIA investing billions unless the technology roadmap has real credibility.
