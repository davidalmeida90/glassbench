## 6. Volume — Distribution Risk

**VWMA = 38.85**, and price (35.52) is **−8.6% below it**. Trading below the volume-weighted average price means recent transacted volume has occurred at higher prices — i.e., new buyers are underwater, which is a distribution/bearish tell.

Volume behavior supports caution: the **2025-11-13 down day (close 35.91, from 37.89) came on 95.6M shares**, well above the ~50–60M typical of early November — a high-volume sell-off. The 2025-11-14 continuation was on lighter volume (71.7M). Meanwhile the Sept–Oct advance featured enormous spikes (e.g., **523.9M on 2025-09-18** and **246.4M on 2025-10-24**), showing the rally was heavily news/event-driven. When high-volume up-moves are followed by high-volume down-days, it flags profit-taking/distribution rather than quiet consolidation.

## 7. Synthesis & Actionable Scenarios

**The core narrative:** INTC is in a **long-term uptrend (above a rising 200 SMA, post-golden-cross) undergoing a sharp, high-volatility short-term correction** from a parabolic peak. The tool data shows the close ran from ~24 (2025-09-17) to **41.53 (2025-10-28)** — a ~+73% move — before retracing to 35.52, which is **about −14.5% from the 2025-10-28 close** (and ~−16.4% from the 2025-10-28 intraday high of 42.48). This is a magnitude typical of a "cooling off" phase, not yet evidence of trend destruction.

**Level map (all from verified/tool data):**
- **Immediate support / decision zone:** 50 SMA **34.63** + Bollinger lower band **35.25** + 2025-11-14 low **34.62** — a tight 34.62–35.25 cluster. This is the line in the sand.
- **Resistance overhead:** 10 EMA **37.42**, then Bollinger middle **38.40**, then VWMA **38.85**. A reclaim of the 10 EMA would be the first sign of stabilization.
- **Deeper support if 34.6 breaks:** With ATR ~1.77, a 2×ATR extension points toward the low-to-mid 30s; the rising 200 SMA (25.05) is the strategic backstop far below.

**Bullish (dip-continuation) case:** Uptrend structure intact (price > 50 > 200 SMA), RSI merely neutral (not overbought), price at the lower Bollinger band and right on the 50 SMA — a logical mean-reversion entry zone *if* the 34.6–35.3 shelf holds. Confirmation trigger: a close back above the 10 EMA (37.42).

**Bearish (correction-deepening) case:** MACD bearish crossover (Oct 30) with widening negative histogram, price below 10 EMA/50 SMA-adjacent/VWMA/Bollinger middle, high-volume down day on 11-13, and RSI with room to fall to oversold. A decisive close below **34.6** invalidates the pullback-holding thesis and opens the mid-30s/lower zone.

**Risk management:** With ATR at ~5% of price, use wider stops (1–2 ATR) and smaller size. A common structure would be a stop just below the 34.6–35.3 confluence, sized so that a stop-out equals a tolerable portfolio loss.
