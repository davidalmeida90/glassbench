- A diversified revenue base across vaccines, oncology, internal medicine, and immunology.

- A pipeline and balance sheet that allow bolt-on M&A and business development.

The bear will point to patent cliffs. Fair. But **PFE** has already been de-rated from the $50–60 COVID-era peak to roughly $25. Much of that patent risk is priced in. The market is not paying a premium multiple for **PFE** anymore. At **1.6x price-to-book, 2.3x price-to-sales, and 3.1x EV/sales**, the stock is cheap relative to its cash generation.

The bear is looking at the rearview mirror of GAAP earnings. The bull is looking at the windshield of cash generation and de-leveraging.
