Volatility is **compressing** (ATR down to 8.15 from ~13.9 in early August), a condition that historically resolves into a directional expansion. The balance of evidence does not yet justify a directional bet — this is a **HOLD / wait-for-confirmation** setup.

**Bullish trigger (go long):**

**Bearish trigger (reduce / short):**

- Confirmation filters: RSI < 40 and MACD making new lows; watch for the 50 SMA crossing below the 200 SMA.
- Downside risk opens toward the 317.49 (7/23) swing-low region; a confirmed death cross would extend the medium-term bear case.

**Risk management:**
- Use **ATR 8.15** to size positions — a 1× ATR adverse move is ~2.4% of price.
- Because the range is narrow (336–347), keep stops tight but outside the noise band; avoid stops placed exactly at the round-number MAs where stop-hunting is common.
- Prefer breakout **confirmation** over prediction given the absence of a momentum edge.

I deliberately avoided redundant pairs (e.g., not selecting RSI + StochRSI, or MACD line + signal + histogram separately) to keep the eight inputs diverse across trend, momentum, volatility, and volume.
