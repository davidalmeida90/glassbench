**Interpretation:** Retail's bearish energy is pointed at AI *labs* and chip/infra froth, and a subset of that thinking (free-cash-flow-rich hyperscalers as relative winners) actually resolves **in GOOGL's favor**. The mismatch therefore reads less as "retail chasing while institutions are cautious" and more as "retail is fearful of the sector, while sector-level evidence is being routed toward GOOGL." That argues against reading this as a contrarian top signal; it does, however, mean near-term price action (a possible Monday AI-infra gap-down) may not track the constructive fundamental news.
