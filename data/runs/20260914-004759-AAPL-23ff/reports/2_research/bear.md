**On AI and competition, your “ecosystem” argument is dangerously complacent.**  
Apple is not behind because it lacks cash. It’s behind because AI is a platform shift, and the leaders—Microsoft/OpenAI, Google, Samsung, Nvidia—are moving faster. Apple’s installed base is sticky, but it is not immune to switching if Siri and Apple Intelligence fail to impress. Services growth, which carries the high margins, faces escalating regulatory pressure: App Store antitrust, EU DMA, DOJ scrutiny. China remains a two-sided risk: Huawei is regaining premium share, and geopolitical/tariff tensions could disrupt both demand and supply chains. These are not minor caveats. They are structural threats to the multiple.
