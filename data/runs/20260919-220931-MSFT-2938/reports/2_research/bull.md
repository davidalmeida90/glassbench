## 4. Bear counterpoints — let’s debate them directly

**Bear: “Rates are at 5%, and Fed hike risk will crush MSFT’s multiple.”**

Correct: FCF fell from $74.1B to $71.6B to **$67.0B**. But that’s because capex exploded from $44.5B to **$115.9B** to build AI infrastructure. Operating cash flow still grew **+34.4% to $182.9B**. I think that’s a bad bet against Azure, Copilot, and OpenAI.

**Bear: “Earnings quality is poor — security gains and deferred tax boosted results.”**

Normalized income still grew **+26.4%**. Operating income grew **+20.8%**. Operating margin expanded to **46.78%**. Those are not accounting tricks. That’s operating leverage.

**Bear: “The stock is extended above the 200-day.”**

It’s 14.8% above the 200-day, but it’s also **10.8% below its 52-week high of $553.72**. RSI is neutral. This is not a blow-off top. It’s a consolidation after a major re-rating.

**Bear: “AI backlash and mega-cap rotation will pressure the stock.”**

AI regulation is a real tail risk, but **MSFT** is diversified across cloud, productivity, gaming, LinkedIn, and Windows. It has navigated antitrust before. As for mega-cap rotation — if money rotates, it will rotate toward quality growth with a 20.9x forward PE, not away from it.
