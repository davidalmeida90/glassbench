## Rating: Hold — NVDA (NVIDIA Corporation, NMS)

### Debate Evaluation

The bull and bear both present credible evidence, but neither side clearly dominates.

**Bull case strengths:**  
- NVDA remains above its 10 EMA, 50 SMA, and 200 SMA, with the 50 SMA rising.  
- RSI reset from 36.73 to 55.53, leaving room before overbought.  
- MACD is recovering, though not yet confirmed.  
- Fundamentals remain exceptional: Q2 FY2026 revenue of $46.743B, 72.4% gross margin, 60.8% operating margin, and massive cash generation.  
- Fortress balance sheet, buybacks, and CUDA ecosystem support the long-term thesis.

**Bear case strengths:**  
- The bounce has produced lower highs: $184.03, $184.02, then $178.85.  
- Volume declined after the Sep 10 gap: 226.85M → 151M → 125M.  
- MACD has not confirmed a bullish crossover; histogram is still negative.  
- QoQ revenue growth is only ~6%, gross and operating margins are compressing.  
- Working capital is building rapidly: inventory up ~48%, receivables up, cash conversion only ~58%.  
- Near-term risk/reward from current price is poor: roughly +4% to $184–$185 resistance versus -6% to $166–$167 support.

The bull’s fundamental case supports holding a long-term core position. The bear’s technical and cash-flow concerns make a new buy here premature. A full sell is also not clearly warranted because the primary trend is not broken and NVDA remains a highly profitable AI compute leader.

**Missing data:** point-in-time valuation multiples were withheld, so no valuation-based conclusion can be drawn.
