## 5. Key Strengths (Bull Evidence)
1. **Hyper-scale profitability:** 60.8% operating margin and 72.4% gross margin in Q2 FY26 — among the highest of any large-cap semiconductor company.
2. **Fortress balance sheet:** ~$43.4B net cash, 10.6% debt-to-equity, 4.21x current ratio. No solvency risk.
3. **Explosive multi-year growth:** Revenue +114% in FY25 on top of +126% in FY24; net income nearly 17x over two years.
4. **Massive FCF engine:** $60.9B FY25 FCF, $13.5B in the latest quarter, comfortably funding ~$10B/quarter of shareholder returns.
5. **Shareholder returns accelerating:** Buybacks stepped up from ~$9.5B (FY24) to $33.7B (FY25), with $9.72B repurchased in Q2 FY26 alone.

## 6. Key Risks (Bear Evidence)
1. **Decelerating sequential growth:** Q2 FY26 revenue of $46.74B vs. Q1 FY26's ~$44.1B implies only ~6% QoQ growth — a marked slowdown from the 3-digit YoY rates.
2. **Gross-margin compression:** 75.0% (FY25) → 72.4% (Q2 FY26), reflecting Blackwell ramp and mix/China headwinds.
3. **Earnings quality:** $2.25B one-time securities gain flatters reported EPS; normalized EPS is materially lower.
4. **Working-capital balloon:** Receivables +$5.68B and inventory +$3.62B QoQ drove an $11.0B cash drag and cut OCF/NI conversion to ~58%. Inventory is up ~48% since FY25-end — a write-down risk.
5. **Geopolitical/export exposure:** China (H20) restrictions remain an overhang not fully visible in these statements.

## 7. Actionable Insights for Traders
- **The core machine is intact but the growth *rate* is normalizing.** Focus on whether Q3 FY26 revenue can re-accelerate above the ~$46.7B base. Any guide-down would pressure the premium multiple.
- **Adjust for the one-timer.** Use the **normalized net income of $24.5B / normalized EPS derived ex-gain** for valuation work rather than the headline $26.4B.
- **Monitor the working-capital cycle.** An $11.0B negative WC swing and 48% inventory build is the single most important emerging risk. A reversal (or a write-down) would directly hit gross margin.
- **The balance sheet provides downside cushion.** ~$43B net cash and $10B/quarter capital returns offer support and flexibility even in a demand wobble.
- **Watch gross margin as the leading indicator.** Sustained sub-72% readings would signal pricing pressure; recovery toward 75% would confirm Blackwell cost normalization.
