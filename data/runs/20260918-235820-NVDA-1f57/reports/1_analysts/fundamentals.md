## 7. Actionable Insights for Traders

1. **Growth is still accelerating, not decelerating.** Revenue compounded ~100%/yr for three years and the most recent quarters show ~20% sequential gains — the fundamental momentum supports the premium multiple. For momentum/trend traders this is a "do-not-fight-the-tape" fundamental backdrop.

2. **Multiple is rich but growth-justified — watch for multiple compression risk.** P/E 45.6x, P/S 25.4x, P/B 34.8x, EV/EBITDA 41.1x. Upside now depends on **continued upward estimate revisions**; any AI-capex digestion or guidance wobble would hit a high-multiple stock disproportionately. Position sizing/stop discipline matters.

3. **Margin trajectory is a key swing factor.** The FY2026 GM dip to 71.1% was driven by the one-time H20 charge; underlying GM is recovering toward ~75%. Confirmation of sustained ~75% GM plus ~65% operating margin would be a bullish confirmation; renewed export-control charges or a Blackwell/next-gen ramp cost overhang would be the bear case.

4. **Balance sheet = downside cushion.** Minimal debt, ~net cash, ~$10.6B cash & current securities, and ~100% ROE. Liquidity/insolvency risk is essentially nil — a "quality" anchor for longer-horizon holders.

5. **Capital return & FCF are the shareholder value engine.** ~$96.7B FCF and ~$48.5B returned in FY2026. Sustained buybacks shrink share count (diluted shares drifting down: 24.5B → 24.4B), mechanically supporting EPS.

6. **Near-term catalyst/risk: the upcoming Q1 FY2027 print (expected late May 2026).** With the reported data through Q4 FY2026 (filed 2026-02-25), the next earnings event is imminent and is the single biggest near-term event risk/reward driver. (The income-statement tool's 2026-04-30 column, if accurate, implies a very strong ~$81.6B quarter and $2.39 EPS — but this is a **potential look-ahead and should not be treated as confirmed as of 2026-05-15**.)

7. **Earnings-quality caveat.** Large, lumpy "other income" (investment gains) is boosting net income in some periods (e.g., $5.6B in Q4 FY2026). Traders modeling forward EPS should anchor on **operating income (~$130B TTM)** rather than headline net income.
